# Target — Shipping & Delivery

## Standard shipping
For items that do not qualify for 2-day shipping, items are expected to be delivered within **3–5 business days** after they have shipped. Standard shipping is free on orders over $35 or on orders placed with a Target Circle Card.

## Order Pickup
Every Target offers online Order Pickup; orders are picked and ready within a couple of hours.

## Drive Up
Items are brought to your car within minutes of arrival at the store.

## Faster options
- 2-Day Shipping and Same Day Delivery are available on eligible items.
- Most items eligible for shipping are eligible for next-day delivery — about 85% of what Target sells in stores.

# Target — Target Circle Card (formerly RedCard)

## Payment options
The Target Circle Card is available as a Target Circle Debit Card, Target Circle Credit Card, or Target Circle Mastercard.

## 5% discount
Using a Target Circle Debit Card, Credit Card, or Mastercard at Target stores or Target.com gives **5% off** purchases.
- If a Target Circle Card is used in the same transaction with another form of payment, the 5% discount applies only to the amount charged to the Target Circle Card.

## Discount exclusions
The 5% discount does not apply to: prescriptions and other CVS pharmacy products; Target GiftCards; Visa, Mastercard, and American Express prepaid cards; sports-betting gift cards; Starbucks gift cards purchased at in-store Starbucks; previous purchases; Target clinic services; and Target Optical eye exams and protection plans, among other exclusions.

## Name change
Target RedCard has been renamed the Target Circle Card. Existing RedCard holders need do nothing and keep the same benefits.

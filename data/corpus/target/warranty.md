# Target — Protection Plans (Allstate)

## Eligible products
Most consumer electronics are eligible for an Allstate Protection Plan, including mobile phones, televisions, projectors, wearable devices, video game consoles, small appliances, cameras, camcorders, laptops/tablets, computers and peripherals. Select baby products, bikes, and furniture are also covered. Pre-owned electronics are **not** covered.

## Coverage
The plan provides parts and labor coverage to repair or replace the item during the term of the plan.

## No Lemon Policy
If a covered product has two service repairs for the same problem and a third repair is needed for the same problem within any twelve-month period, the product is replaced with a comparable product, or a cash settlement is provided as a Target GiftCard or check.

## Registration and claims
Registration is not required until a claim is filed; register online at allstateprotectionplans.com/service. For help, visit allstateprotectionplans.com/user/login or call a myTGTtech agent at 1-877-698-4883.

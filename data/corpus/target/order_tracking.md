# Target — Track Order

## How to track
On Target.com, click your name in the upper-right corner and select **Purchase History** to view orders, order status, and tracking information. In the Target app, select **My Target** (lower right), then **Purchase history**.

## Timing
Tracking information can take up to **24 hours** to appear after an item ships. Target sends email updates as it processes the order, including carrier tracking details for shipped orders.

## Notifications
Enable notifications in the Target app to receive status updates, including for Drive Up and Order Pickup items.

## Need help
Call Target.com Guest Services at 1-800-591-3869.

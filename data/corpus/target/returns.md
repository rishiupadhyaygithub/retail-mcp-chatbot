# Target — Returns

## Standard return window
Most unopened items sold by Target in new condition and returned within **90 days** receive a refund or exchange.

## Electronics and entertainment
- Electronics and entertainment items (excluding Apple and Beats products) must be returned or exchanged within **30 days**.
- All Apple and Beats products (including mobile phones) must be returned or exchanged within **14 days**.

## Registry and Wish List returns
- Baby and Wedding registries have up to **one year** after the event date to return most new, unopened items.
- Wish Lists have up to **90 days** after the purchase date to return unopened items.

## How to return
- **In store:** bring the item plus receipt, return barcode, or original payment card to Guest Services.
- **Online:** go to "My Purchases" on Target.com or the app, select "Return," and follow the prompts. A return label may be printed for mail-in returns.

## Refund methods and timing
Refunds typically go to the original payment method. Timing varies:
- Target Circle Card: up to 2 days.
- Third-party credit cards: up to 5 days.
- Mail-in returns: 7–10 additional business days of processing after the item is received.

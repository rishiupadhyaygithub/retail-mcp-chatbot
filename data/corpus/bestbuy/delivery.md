# Best Buy — Shipping, Delivery & Store Pickup

## Free shipping
Best Buy Plus and Best Buy Total members receive free 2-day shipping. For non-members, a **$35 minimum** order is required to qualify for free shipping; orders under $35 may incur a shipping fee.

## Store pickup
Most store pickup orders are ready within an hour. Items are held at the store for **5 days** before the order is automatically canceled if not picked up.

## Ship-to-store
If an item isn't available at your local store, ship-to-store items usually arrive within **3 to 7 business days**.

## Same-day delivery and curbside
Same-day delivery is available seven days a week for eligible products. Curbside pickup is free.

## Alternate pickup locations
Orders can be picked up at alternate locations including FedEx Office, FedEx Ship Center, Walgreens, Dollar General, Office Depot, and OfficeMax.

# Best Buy — Return & Exchange Policy

## Standard return window
Most products can be returned or exchanged within **15 days** for a full refund. The return period begins the day you receive your product and applies to new, clearance, open-box, refurbished, and pre-owned products.

## Membership extensions
Best Buy Plus and Best Buy Total members have a return window of **60 days**.

## Category-specific windows
- Cell phones, cellular tablets, mobile hotspots, and cellular wearables: **14 days**.
- Pet training devices and hearing aids: **60 days**.
- Litter boxes: **90 days**.
- Halloween Decorations and Christmas Decorations categories: **15 days**.

## How to return
Bring the item with all contents, packaging, and proof of purchase to a store, and it will be processed for return or exchange.

## Refund timing (returns by mail)
Once a mailed return is received, it is processed within **7–10 business days**. Depending on the bank's processing time, it may take up to 10 days after processing to reflect on the account.

## Restocking fees
A **$45 restocking fee** applies to Activatable Devices (cell phones, cellular tablets, mobile hotspots, and cellular wearables), excluding prepaid phones. There is no restocking fee if the product is unopened, or for purchases made in AL, CO, HI, IA, MS, OH, OK, and SC, or where prohibited by law. The fee is deducted from the refund where it applies.

# Best Buy — Geek Squad Protection & Manufacturer Warranty

## Coverage overview
The Geek Squad Protection Plan supplements the manufacturer's warranty. If something goes wrong after the original manufacturer warranty ends, Geek Squad Protection covers qualified repairs.

## Interaction with the manufacturer's warranty
- Parts and coverage available under the manufacturer's warranty are **not** covered by the Plan during the manufacturer's warranty period.
- You can file a claim directly with the manufacturer for issues covered by the manufacturer's warranty.
- Geek Squad Protection often covers repairs the manufacturer warranty does not, such as failure due to a power surge, even during the manufacturer's warranty period.

## Replacement coverage
If the manufacturer replaces your product, the replacement continues to be covered by the Plan, but you must contact Best Buy with the serial number and other requested information so the new device is identified as the covered product.

# Best Buy — Payment Options & Financing

## Payment methods
Best Buy cardmembers can choose rewards or flexible financing on purchases. Flexible payment options require no credit for purchases **$225 and up**, letting customers split purchases into installments. Gift cards can be used anytime, in store or online.

## Best Buy credit cards
My Best Buy cardmembers can choose rewards or flexible financing on qualifying purchases. Cardholders get **5% back in rewards** on Best Buy purchases with standard credit, with the option to bank their points.

## Financing
With My Best Buy credit cards, customers can use deferred-interest financing offers: interest is deferred for a set period, and paying the full balance before the promotional term ends avoids interest on that purchase.

## Card account payments
Cardmembers can pay online via Citibank Account Online, use the automated voice system at 1-888-574-1301, or text PAY to 81964 from the phone linked to the account. In-store staff can also process payments.

## Gift cards
Best Buy gift cards cannot be applied toward payment on a Best Buy Credit Card balance.

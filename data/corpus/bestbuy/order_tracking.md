# Best Buy — Track Your Order

## How to track
Check the tracking link in your shipping confirmation email, or sign in to your BestBuy.com account to check order status. From your account, go to **Order Status**, locate the order, and click **Track Package**. On the Order Details page, the **Track Package** button opens the package-tracking page with shipping status details.

## Guest orders
Orders placed without signing in can be found via the Online Order Lookup using the order number. To track future orders automatically, sign in to your account during checkout.

## Still can't find it
Call 1-888-BEST BUY (1-888-237-8289) with your order number ready for faster service.

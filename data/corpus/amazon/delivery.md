# Amazon — Shipping Rates & Times

## Free shipping
Orders shipping to an eligible destination with at least the stated minimum threshold of eligible items qualify for Free Shipping by Amazon. Some items shipped to Alaska or Hawaii may not be eligible for free shipping. With Extended Free Shipping, after placing a qualifying free-shipping order you can use free shipping for the next **24 hours** without an order minimum.

## Shipping rates and times
Rates and times depend on the shipping speed and the weight/size of the items. Two-Day Delivery takes **two business days** after an item ships. Same-Day Delivery is available to select zip codes, with rates differing for Prime and non-Prime customers.

## Determining your rates
At checkout, select or add a shipping address, choose a shipping speed, and the total shipping & handling cost and estimated delivery date are listed under Order Summary.

# Amazon — Refund Timelines

## After the return is received
Once the item is received and processed at Amazon's fulfillment center, it takes about **2 business days** to process the refund and **3–5 business days** for the refund amount to appear in your account.

## Overall timeline
Allow up to **30 days** to receive a refund, as return and refund timelines vary due to factors including the refund payment method. This longer timeframe accounts for the time it takes the item to reach Amazon.

## Payment method variation
Your bank may need extra time to process a refund. Different payment methods process at different speeds, so the exact timeline varies depending on how you originally paid.

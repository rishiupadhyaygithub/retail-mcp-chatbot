# Amazon — Manufacturer's Warranties & Repairs

## Manufacturer warranties
Manufacturer warranties may not apply in all cases, depending on factors such as how the product is used, where it was purchased, and who it was purchased from. For questions, contact the manufacturer.

## Third-party seller products
Each seller handles warranty claims for its own products. Go to your order history, select **View Order Details**, find **Sold by**, click the seller's name, and use **Ask a Question** to contact them.

## Amazon-brand products (Amazon Basics)
Amazon Basics products are warranted against defects in materials and workmanship under ordinary consumer use for **one year** from the date of original retail purchase. If a product is defective within the warranty period, Amazon will, at its option, replace, repair, or refund it.

## How to obtain service
For Amazon's own products, contact customer service, which may ask questions to confirm eligibility. If eligible, a replacement order or refund is issued with instructions for returning the defective product postage-paid. Amazon device warranties and service requests can be managed through the Manage Your Content and Devices page.

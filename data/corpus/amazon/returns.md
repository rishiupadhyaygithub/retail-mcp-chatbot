# Amazon — Return Policy

## Standard return window
Most items can be returned for a refund or replacement/exchange within **30 days** of delivery, as long as they are in original or unused condition.

## Condition requirements
Items must be returned in their original or unused condition. Some categories have specific condition rules; opened or used items may be subject to a partial refund.

## How to return
Use the Amazon Online Returns Center to start a return for most items within 30 days of delivery. Select the order, choose a reason, and pick a drop-off or pickup method. A prepaid return label is generated for eligible items.

## Category-specific windows
Some categories differ from the 30-day standard (for example, certain devices and fashion items have their own terms). Non-returnable items (such as some hazardous materials, digital content, and personalized goods) cannot be returned.

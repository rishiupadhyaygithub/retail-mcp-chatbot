# IKEA US — Payment Options & Financing

## In-store payment
IKEA accepts cash, credit/debit cards (Visa, Discover, American Express, Mastercard), IKEA gift cards, store return cards, Apple Pay, and Google Pay at physical stores.

## Online and phone payment
For online and phone orders, IKEA accepts IKEA gift/return/refund cards (with PIN), the IKEA Projekt card, American Express, Discover, Mastercard, Visa, Afterpay, and PayPal.

## Gift and refund cards
IKEA gift cards, refund cards, vouchers, coupons, and discount codes are all accepted. Gift and debit/credit cards can be used online or in store.

## Financing
- **Afterpay:** splits a purchase into four interest-free payments — the first at checkout and the remaining three every two weeks.
- **IKEA Projekt Credit Card:** 0% interest on purchases of **$500 or more for 6 months**; accepted online and in store as a financing and payment method.
- IKEA also offers internal financing through IKEA Family and the IKEA Projekt Card.

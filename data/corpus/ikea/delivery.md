# IKEA US — Delivery

## Small order shipping (continental US)
For items like textiles, decorations, and accessories. IKEA Family members save $5 on qualifying small orders over $35. Minimum $35 merchandise subtotal; maximum weight 50 lbs. No signature required. Cannot deliver to PO Boxes or APO addresses.

## Hawaii shipping
Shipping to select Hawaii zip codes starts at $80, with the same minimums and restrictions as the continental US.

## Standard delivery
IKEA Family pricing starts at $19; non-members start at $29. Delivery is made within the date range you choose, for orders under 300 lbs. Rates vary by location, with higher rates in select northeastern states ($39–$49).

## Scheduled doorstep delivery
IKEA Family pricing starts at $29; non-members start at $39. The independent delivery provider notifies you of a 4-hour time window. Can be scheduled as early as tomorrow.

## Scheduled in-home delivery
IKEA Family pricing starts at $59; non-members start at $69. Delivers to one room of choice. Mattress setup and packaging removal included.

## Express in-home delivery
IKEA Family pricing starts at $69; non-members start at $79. Available in selected zip codes. Delivery as early as tomorrow.

## Key requirements
- Minimum $35 merchandise subtotal.
- Someone 18+ must be present for large-item delivery.
- Orders over 500 lbs require Scheduled or Express In-Home Delivery.
- Not all shipping and delivery options are available in all zip codes.

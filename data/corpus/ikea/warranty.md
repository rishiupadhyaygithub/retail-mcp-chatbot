# IKEA US — Guarantees & Warranties

## Kitchen installation
Local professional kitchen installers provide warranties for **5 years** post-completion on IKEA-supplied kitchen products. Coverage applies to installation work by licensed, insured installers.

## Appliances
Appliance coverage runs **365 days** from purchase. For appliance help outside of 365 days, customers are directed to contact Whirlpool directly. A dedicated Product Warranties page covers longer limited-warranty terms on specific ranges.

## Spare parts availability
Products purchased within 365 days may qualify for replacement-part assistance. Parts with 6-digit numbers can be ordered through the self-service spare-parts portal.

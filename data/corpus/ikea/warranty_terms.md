# IKEA US — Limited Warranties by Product Range

IKEA provides written limited warranties on specific product ranges, in addition to the standard returns policy. Terms below are the published guarantee lengths per range. All limited warranties apply to **residential (household) use only** and cover defects in material and workmanship from the date of purchase, with proof of purchase (original receipt).

## Kitchens (SEKTION system)
The SEKTION kitchen system carries a **25-year** limited warranty. It covers the function and quality of kitchen cabinet frames, fronts, drawers, hinges, and shelves against defects in material and workmanship. It does not cover normal wear and tear, damage from misuse, or non-household use.

## Kitchen faucets
IKEA kitchen faucets carry a **10-year** limited warranty covering defects in material and workmanship for residential use. It covers the faucet's finish, cartridge, and other functional parts against manufacturing defects.

## Sofas and armchairs
The limited warranty for sofas and armchairs remains in force for **10 years** from the date of purchase, covering the frame, seat cushion foam, and seat cushion springs against defects. The **STOCKHOLM** sofa range carries an extended **25-year** limited warranty. Loose covers and normal wear are excluded.

## Mattresses
The limited warranty for mattresses remains in force for **10 years** from the date of purchase. It covers defects in material and workmanship in all mattresses, including split seams, damaged springs, broken handles, broken mattress base, and substantial sagging. It does not cover changes in the comfort or firmness of the mattress over time, or a change of mind (which is instead handled by the 90-day one-time mattress exchange under the returns policy).

## What the limited warranties cover
- Defects in material and workmanship, as described per range above.
- Repair, or if repair is not possible, replacement of the defective product with the same or a comparable product.

## What is excluded
- Products used commercially or outside the home.
- Normal wear and tear, cuts, scratches, or damage caused by impacts or accidents.
- Damage from improper assembly, misuse, alteration, or exposure to conditions outside normal residential use.
- Consumable or perishable parts unless a material defect is present.

## How to make a claim
Contact IKEA customer service with proof of purchase (the original receipt). IKEA will examine the product and decide whether to repair or replace it under the applicable range warranty. The warranty does not extend the standard returns window; it runs separately from the day of purchase.

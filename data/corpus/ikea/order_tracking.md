# IKEA US — Track & Manage Order

## How to track an order
Visit the Track order page (https://www.ikea.com/us/en/purchases/lookup/). You need:
- Your order number
- The email or phone number used to complete the purchase

If you logged in with your IKEA Family account when ordering, you can see all previous orders through your profile.

## What you can do
- Track an online order and the status of the delivery.
- View order details (items purchased, time purchased, delivery information, estimated arrival).
- Download a receipt for store orders.
- Reschedule delivery.

## Order status stages
- **Preparing order:** received and will be processed; you can still cancel and change the delivery date.
- **Picking order:** being processed and completed; too late to cancel, but you can return unwanted items once received.
- **Preparing delivery:** your order is in transit.
- **On its way:** sent from the dispatcher and on its way to you.
- **Delivered:** successfully delivered.
